# Faretheewellness
Healing doesn't have to hurt.

## About Heal Your Face

Heal Your Face exists for victims of interconnected crises including homelessness, addiction, depression, violence, bullying, self-harm, suicide, hunger, trauma, and identity collapse. 

### Our Approach

We meet people where they are and respect adult autonomy in defining health. We are open to evidence-based and emerging treatments under supervision.

### Important Notice

**HYF does not provide medical care or replace licensed professionals.** We support individuals in their healing journey while working alongside, not in place of, qualified healthcare providers.
